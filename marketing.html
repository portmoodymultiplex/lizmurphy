Marketing

Marketing with care.

Every home Liz lists gets the full strength of Zada Group's marketing — photography, targeted campaigns, custom websites, print — delivered quietly, beautifully, and with respect for the home and the family behind it.

Targeted Social Media

Finding the right buyer, kindly.

A recent Zada Group listing campaign reached 1,622 viewers — 88% of them beyond the existing follower base. The campaigns are built to find the people most likely to love a home, not to shout the loudest.

Every New West listing receives a custom campaign, with creative, copy, and targeting tuned to the property — and to the buyer who'll care for it next.

Custom Listing Websites

Some homes deserve their own address online too.

Distinctive and higher-end listings get a dedicated single-property website — its own domain, a cinematic hero, and a full gallery. Buyers remember it, share it with family, and return to it. For a home that held a lifetime, it doubles as a lovely keepsake.

One of the marketing tools available to every Zada Group seller.

Photography & Print

Photographed gently. Printed beautifully.

Every listing is professionally photographed — with drone work where it helps — and supported by a custom printed brochure, presented at showings and mailed to surrounding neighbours. For many of Liz's clients and their buyers, paper still matters. She agrees.

Showings, Considerately

Marketing that respects the people still living there.

Many of Liz's listings are homes where someone still lives — sometimes someone for whom showings are genuinely disruptive. Schedules are built around naps, nurses, and routines. Open houses happen when they help, not by default. The home is presented at its best without the family ever feeling displaced from it.
