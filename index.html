When the move is bigger than a house.

Liz Murphy is a Seniors Real Estate Specialist® on the Zada Group team. Downsizing after decades in one home, selling on a parent's behalf, or trading the rain for a second coast, she's the calm person families call when a move carries more than boxes.

Listen first. Then decide.

Liz works as a senior agent on Zada Group, licensed with Stonehaus Realty Corp. Her practice spans first-time buyers through luxury, but its deepest roots are with seniors and their families: people working through a sale, a downsize, or a power-of-attorney conversation, often all at once. Her approach is the same every time. Listen first, explain things slowly, and give people the version of the truth that helps them decide.

Patient guidance for every chapter.

Some moves are exciting. Some are tender. Liz has built her practice around the ones that need a calm person in the room, and she brings the same care to all of them.

Downsizing with dignity

Leaving a home after twenty or forty years isn't a transaction, it's a transition. Liz helps seniors sort through the options actually available to them, at a pace that respects the weight of the decision.

Families and power of attorney

When a parent can no longer make decisions for themselves, the family suddenly faces a sale, a move and a legal process at the same time. Liz walks families through it gently, clearly and without pressure.

Estate and executor sales

Selling a home on behalf of someone who is gone asks more of everyone involved. Liz handles these sales with discretion: the paperwork done properly, the home treated with respect, the family given room to breathe.

Retirement and rightsizing

Single-level living, a lock-and-leave condo, a home closer to the grandkids. Buying for the next chapter is its own kind of search, and Liz frames it around how you want to live, not just bedrooms and square footage.

The second coast

For clients ready to sell and move to Mexico, split the year, or invest on the coast. Liz lived in Bucerías for seven years and works with the brokerage her family started there. See the program.

First moves to forever homes

The seniors focus doesn't narrow her practice, it defines its character. Liz works with first-time buyers through luxury, and the work is the same: listen first, explain clearly, never rush a decision that deserves time.
