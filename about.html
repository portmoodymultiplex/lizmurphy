About

Liz Murphy.

Mom of three. Seniors Real Estate Specialist®. Seven years on the coast of Mexico. The REALTOR® families call when the conversation is bigger than a house.

Liz Murphy
Realtor · SRES® · Zada Group
liz@zadagroup.ca 604.365.9369 @lizmurphyrealtor

Liz Murphy is a mom of three with a real estate career that started outside of Canada and has come full circle. She spent seven years living in Bucerías, Mexico, where she and her father built Bienvenidos Real Estate and she learned how transactions look when you're working in a different language, a different legal system, and with clients who are usually a long way from home. That experience left her with a steadiness most agents don't develop until much later, and a real soft spot for clients doing something that feels enormous and unfamiliar.

Back in BC, she works across Greater Vancouver as a senior agent on the Zada Group team, licensed with Stonehaus Realty Corp. And through Bienvenidos, she helps clients who are ready to sell here and start again on the coast of Mexico, with both ends of the move handled by people who actually talk to each other.

Where Liz has quietly built the deepest part of her practice is with seniors and their families. She's the realtor people call when a parent can no longer make decisions for themselves and the family is suddenly facing a sale, a downsize, a power-of-attorney conversation , or all three at once. It's emotional work. It involves a lot of waiting in living rooms, a lot of explaining things slowly, and a lot of being the calm person in a room of people who are not feeling calm. Liz is exceptionally empathetic by nature, and she's made a real practice out of helping seniors understand the options actually available to them, instead of being pushed toward whatever's fastest. She is SRES® (Seniors Real Estate Specialist®) designated.

That depth doesn't narrow her client base. Liz works with first-time buyers all the way up through luxury, and she'll tell you the work is the same: listen first, then give people the version of the truth that helps them decide.

Outside of work, you'll usually find her with her kids, or planning the next trip south.
