The Second Coast.

Downsizing, relocating and investing on Mexico's Riviera Nayarit. Liz handles the BC side. Bienvenidos Real Estate, the Bucerías brokerage her family founded, handles the Mexico side. Both ends of the move, talking to each other.

In 2004, I packed up my life and moved to Mexico. For seven years we lived the life most people only get on vacation.

I opened a gym in Bucerías, my kids went to school there, and real estate found me almost by accident. My dad was a REALTOR® here in Canada and had started a website for property in the area. Canadians kept contacting him, so one day he called and said we should be doing these deals together. So we did. In 2010 we opened a brick-and-mortar office under the name Bienvenidos Real Estate.

My dad ran it until 2019, when he sold it to Andres (Andy) Cabrera, who had been with the firm since 2013. Bienvenidos is still going strong, and the relationships we built there are very much alive.

Today I'm a REALTOR® back home in BC, and I've come full circle. I help Canadians sell their homes here and connect them with Bienvenidos in Bucerías, so both ends of the move are handled by people who actually talk to each other.

I speak the language. I know the neighbourhoods, and I know which "ocean view" requires a ladder. If you're dreaming about Mexico, I've lived that dream. Let me help you do it right.

Three ways people do this.

Before you look at a single listing, get clear on what kind of Mexico life you actually want. Most of the people Liz works with land in one of these.

One

Sell everything and go.

Sell in BC, make Mexico home base, and put the difference to work. More freedom and usually more money in your pocket. Liz plans the BC sale around the move, because selling before you leave is almost always simpler than selling from Mexico.

Two

Downsize and split the year.

Rightsize here, buy a condo there, and spend the grey months on the beach. You keep a home base near the grandkids and the doctors you know, with the rules on MSP, vacancy and insurance mapped out before you go.

Three

Invest on the coast.

Buy a place you'll use some of the year and rent the rest. High season runs roughly November to April, and Bienvenidos manages rentals in-house, so the property earns while you're in BC.

The Riviera Nayarit.

The coastline running north from Puerto Vallarta, about five hours nonstop from YVR. This is where Bienvenidos works, and where Liz lived.

Bucerías

Cobblestone streets and a long sandy beach. The town Liz called home, and Bienvenidos' home base.

La Cruz de Huanacaxtle

A working fishing village with a marina and a Sunday market.

Punta de Mita

The northern point of the bay. Gated communities, golf, and the top end of the market.

Sayulita

Surf town, colourful and busy. Strong for rentals, less so for quiet.

San Pancho

Sayulita's calmer neighbour, with a close-knit arts and community scene.

Nuevo Vallarta

Resort-style condos and wide beaches, closest to the airport.

Flamingos

Golf course living between Nuevo Vallarta and Bucerías.

Guayabitos

Further up the coast, a sheltered bay with a slower pace and lower entry prices.

How it works.

One plan for both ends of the move. Liz stays your point person in BC from the first call to the day you get your keys.

  1. A Mexico Move call

    Free, unhurried, no obligation. Your goals, your timeline, and which path fits.

  2. A plan for your BC home

    What it could sell for, whether to sell first or buy first, and the questions to take to your accountant before you decide.

  3. A season on the ground

    If you haven't spent a full season in Bucerías, rent first. A winter in town tells you more than any guide.

  4. An introduction to Bienvenidos

    Liz connects you with the team in Bucerías for tours, listings and local advice.

  5. A safe purchase

    Deposit into third-party escrow, a notario you trust, a fideicomiso set up properly, and title insurance. Never a wire to anyone directly.

  6. Keys, and someone to watch the place

    Rentals and property management through Bienvenidos, so the condo is cared for while you're home.
